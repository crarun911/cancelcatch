Common questions

Will you book the appointment for me?
No — we are a notification service. We monitor availability and send you an instant alert with a direct link. You log in to the VFS portal yourself and book the appointment. This keeps you fully compliant with visa centre rules.
How quickly will I get alerted?
Standard plan users get priority alerts. As soon as our system detects a matching slot, we send you a WhatsApp message and email within seconds. Visa slots disappear fast — having WhatsApp on Standard gives you the best chance.
How does the refund policy work?
Full refund any time before your first alert is sent. Once your first alert reaches you, the service is fulfilled. Contact billing@cancelcatch.co.uk within 48 hours if you have any issues.
Which countries do you cover?
We currently cover Italy, France, Spain, Portugal, Netherlands, Germany, Greece, Austria, Denmark, Switzerland, Norway and Croatia from London, Manchester, Birmingham, Edinburgh and Cardiff VFS centres. More being added regularly.
Can I change my plan later?
Yes — upgrade or downgrade any time from your account page. Changes take effect immediately.

Privacy, Terms & Refund Policy

Last updated: 28 May 2026 · Registered with ICO

1. Who we are

CancelCatch is a visa appointment notification service operated at cancelcatch.co.uk. We are a notification service — we monitor publicly visible appointment availability and alert you. We never book, hold, or manage appointments on your behalf. Contact: privacy@cancelcatch.co.uk

2. What we do

We monitor VFS Global and embassy appointment portals for publicly visible cancellation slots. When a slot matching your preferences appears, we send you an instant WhatsApp or email notification with a direct link. You log in to the official portal yourself and book the appointment. We never access your visa centre account.
